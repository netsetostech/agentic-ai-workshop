The rupees, and the ceiling for the whole lesson

The rates this page prices with (read off the pricing pages on 21 September 2026 - re-verify before you quote them to anyone)

Document AI bills per page: the Layout Parser, which RESIDENCY=us - the Makefile's default - selects, at $10 per 1,000 pages; Enterprise Document OCR, which RESIDENCY=india selects in Mumbai (docai.tf, parser.PROCESSORS), at $1.50 per 1,000 pages. text-embedding-005 bills per character: $0.000025 per 1,000 online. ☷

The bank passbook. You do not decide whether a cheque cleared by remembering that you deposited it; you get the passbook printed and read the line - date, amount, balance. make sources is the passbook of the corpus: one line per document, what version is current, since which generation, what the last entry cost. When someone says "the handbook is updated", the answer is a printed line, not a feeling.

What just happened

One object landed under acme/, Cloud Storage published its record, Pub/Sub pushed it, and the worker won the claim on documents/acme_5b1c9e..., cut three chunks, scanned them once, embedded all three, wrote them staged, swapped them current, retired nothing, wrote sources/acme~gratuity_amendment_2026.md, refreshed ledger/acme and said so in one line. The date came from the document, not from you.

About Rs 915 on the default lane, about Rs 145 in Mumbai, and in both cases Document AI is more than ninety per cent of it; the embeddings for 2,757 chunks cost less than a plate of vada pav. If a line never comes

Three places, in order: gcloud logging read "$W AND jsonPayload.event=\"ingest_failed\"", whose error field is the diagnosis; make dlq, where a message that failed twelve times is parked; and gcloud pubsub subscriptions describe documind-ingest-push - the push endpoint must be the worker's URL and the OIDC account documind-ingest-sa. The two grants in eventarc.tf that let Cloud Storage publish and Pub/Sub mint that token are the ones that fail silently.

What just happened

Thirty-one objects, thirty-one claims, thirty-one ingest_ok lines, all reused=0. The worker paid Document AI by the page for thirteen PDFs, DLP scanned about 3.5 MB of text for nothing, and the embeddings for 2,757 chunks cost about Rs 7. The ANN tier followed a few minutes behind the rows and holds one datapoint per chunk with the restricts tenant_id, kind, doc_type, current. The ledger has 22 acme rows and one fingerprint.

That red gate is the demo, not a bug: vr-01 is the ledger's own golden row (must_contain 60, must_not_contain "90 days"), and in a real release the commit that re-issues the handbook moves lk-06 and vr-01 to 90. The lesson is the ratio, 2 of 283; for a PDF Act the same ratio saves embeddings while Document AI bills every page again.

What just happened

Same name, new bytes, new doc_key. The worker matched every new chunk against the current rows of this source by chunk_hash and the embedding stamp, copied 281 vectors, embedded 2, wrote 283 rows staged, flipped them current in one pass and retired the old 283 with a 30-day expire_at (lesson 3.4 reads swap_versions). The line said reused=281 embedded=2 retired=283, the ledger row moved to revision 2 with its date, the fingerprint changed, the answer said 90, and the scoped gate went red because the golden rows had not moved with the document.

The batch lane, honestly

A PDF over MAX_INLINE_PAGES (250, main.py line 100) is not parsed on the push path: its page count is read off the page tree by pypdf for nothing, ingest_batch/{doc_key} is written, the claim is set to queued, the worker logs ingest_queued_batch with the consumer named and acks. The consumer is documind-ingest-batch, a Cloud Run job on the same image (batch.tf) that runs the worker's own index_document(..., lane="batch") with a 7,200 s timeout, started by the worker as it queues (when BATCH_JOB names it) and hourly at :15 IST regardless. The corpus never takes this lane: its largest file is the CGST Act at 236 pages (real_sources.json; main.py's own comment says "under two hundred", which is wrong by thirty-six pages and right about the consequence). So on this lane make queued prints 0 queued document(s), and to see the lane you must lower the ceiling and give the worker a version it has not seen.

The one rule to remember

Every drill on this page was a line you predicted and then read. A version is its bytes' hash, so the same bytes are never indexed twice and the undo is the old bytes again. A chunk is its text's hash, so a one-clause edit embeds one clause. Nothing on the lane deletes, so the undo costs nothing and the walk can repair storage absence. A person's decision is the one thing no job repairs. Keep those four, and every number here is one you can predict.
