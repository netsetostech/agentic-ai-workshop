Dimension 3 · Cost

Cost: a claim, a month and a floor, at 1k, 10k and 100k claims

Model, retrieval, judge and floor, in rupees and dollars and dated; the ceiling no claim exceeds, and your lane's own average beside it.

Definition

A claim bills four things. The model: the generator's tokens at shared/prices.py's rates for %%GEN_MODEL%%, $%%R_IN%% in and $%%R_OUT%% out per million. The tokens come from the kit's own budget. TokenBudget.fit() splits max_context_tokens, %%TOTAL%% tokens by the kit's estimate, into %%FIXED%% of fixed prompt for this page's claim and %%CHUNKS%% for the passages, so no prompt is packed past %%TOTAL%%; max_answer_tokens caps the answer and its thinking at %%ANSWER%%. That makes %%TOTAL%% in and %%ANSWER%% out the ceiling of one attempt, Rs %%GEN_RS%% a claim; the model's own count differs from the estimate by a little (lesson 3.4). A first attempt that runs out of room is tried once more with three times the room and both are billed; the log says generation_truncated, and a lane whose reserve is right has none. Retrieval: one embedding of the claim, %%EMBED_MODEL%% at $%%EMBED_USD_1K%% per 1,000 characters, at most Rs %%EMBED_RS%% for the longest claim the route accepts; and one Ranking API query for the pool of %%POOL%%, at $%%RANK_USD_1K%% per 1,000 queries of up to %%RANK_DOCS%% documents, Rs %%RANK_RS%%. Together, Rs %%RET_RS%%. The judge: %%N_METRICS_WORD%% autorater calls on each claim it reads, at Gemini 2.0 Flash's $%%JUDGE_IN%% in and $%%JUDGE_OUT%% out per million, which is how Gen AI Evals bills its default autorater: Rs %%JUDGE_RS%% a claim read, at the ceiling, counting each metric's verdict as long as the answer, which is an assumption and not a measurement. The quote check costs nothing. The floor: what the lane bills by the hour with no claim at all, which lessons 0.1 and 0.4 price at Mumbai's list prices: Rs %%FLOOR_SMALL%% a month on a SMALL index, Rs %%FLOOR_MEDIUM%% on MEDIUM. Of that, the index and the connector that documind-api's deploy names come to Rs %%API_FLOOR_SMALL%% on SMALL and Rs %%API_FLOOR_MEDIUM%% on MEDIUM; the desk uses none of the rest.

Dimension 3 · Cost at 10×

Cost at 10×: the limits the kit declares, and the one it does not

Ten times the bill is arithmetic. What stops being linear is a ceiling: %%CONC%% at a time on %%MAXI%% instances, %%REPLICA%% replica, %%RPM%% a minute.

Definition

The commonest 2 on this dimension, the rubric says, scales the bill by ten and stops. At ten times the load something stops being linear, and the kit declares where in its files: an instance count and a concurrency in the API's deploy, a replica count on the index, a quota on Gemini calls. The arithmetic takes the 730-hour month the price pages use and asks each ceiling one question: how much busier than the average minute would the busiest minute have to be for it to bind?

So what breaks first at 10×? Take the top scale and multiply it: a million claims a month is %%PER_MIN_1M%% a minute on average. The first ceiling the kit declares is quota.tf's %%RPM%% generation calls a minute, and it is off. With GEMINI_QUOTA_OVERRIDE at its default the project has no cap of its own on generation calls, and a runaway spends at whatever rate Google's project quota allows. Switched on, it binds when the busiest minute is %%QUOTA_X_1M%% times the average, and every Gemini caller in the project shares it: the chat service's brains, the router, the Desk's checks. The instances are much further away at the SLO, where %%SLOTS%% slots turn over %%TURN_SLO%% claims a minute at %%SLO_S%% s each. At the %%TIMEOUT%% s cut they turn over only %%TURN_CUT%%, which is under the quota: once claims take longer than %%CROSS_S%% s, the slots bind first, at a busiest minute %%CUT_X_1M%% times the average. And the one replica has no number at all. One node answers every claim's search, its capacity is declared nowhere in the kit, and the honest answer at 10× is to measure it before the load arrives.

The bottom of the range is not linear either. With no instance kept warm, a claim that arrives after a quiet spell waits for an instance to start, and at 1k claims a month a claim arrives every %%GAP_1K_MIN%% minutes on average; at 100k, every %%GAP_100K_S%% seconds. --cpu-boost shortens the start; only a floor of one instance, which bills by the hour, removes it.

Dimension 4 · Evals

Evals: what blocks, what advises, and what cannot fail

Computed checks block, the judge advises, and %%N_ISO%% rows assert what the retriever already guarantees.

Definition

The kit's gate is evals/run_eval.py: %%N_THRESHOLDS%% thresholds and %%N_REQUIRED%% required rows over %%N_GOLDEN%% golden rows (%%N_ANSWERABLE%% answerable), scored by code, green or red. The judge reads the same answers and explains them; its docstring calls it the other judge, and its last line says the gate decides. The fact desk keeps that split and adds one check to the blocking side. The quote check runs on every claim before a verdict is written, and as a threshold in the desk's own eval, because the passage it checks against is the very text the model read. The kit's live eval measures quote support against the tenant's whole corpus and reports it as information only, because a Doc AI extraction and a pypdf mirror hyphenate differently; the desk has no second copy to disagree with.

Then the rubric's hardest point: which assertions cannot fail. Of the %%N_GOLDEN%% golden rows, %%N_NEVER%% carry must_not_contain, and %%N_ISO%% of those are isolation rows. They are vacuous: the retriever filters by tenant before the generator reads anything, so another tenant's figure can appear only if the model invents it, which check_isolation() calls a hallucination lottery. What replaced them is isolation_403_rate at 1.00, asked by documind-outsider-sa, an account on no tenant's roster. The one version row is not vacuous: its figure lives only in a revision under evals/demo, and a version the ledger failed to retire can say it. For the desk, one more check would be vacuous: asserting that a quote is at most %%DRAFT_QUOTE_MAX%% characters, which ModelDraft and _draft() enforce before any eval could see it. The %%QUOTE_WORDS%%-word rule is not vacuous, because only the prompt asks for it, so quote_holds() keeps it. Lesson 4.1, next, builds the rows an eval like this is made of.
